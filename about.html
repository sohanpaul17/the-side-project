Founder & Maker

It started as aside project.

The Side Project began the way most good things do — late at night, with a notebook, a cup of chai and a head full of ideas that didn't fit anywhere else. I'd make a planner for myself, a print for a friend's wall, a little invitation for a family party… and people kept asking, “can you make one for me?”

So I did. Today it's a small, one-person studio making customised Lovebooks — little illustrated books about the people you love most. Every product is designed by me, checked by me, and sent by me — whether you want it printed on paper or as a PDF you can use straight away.

When you order, you're not talking to a checkout page. You're talking to me. Say hi any time — I genuinely love hearing what you're making.

— The Maker
